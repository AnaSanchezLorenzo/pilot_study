# pilot_study

Repository for pilot study analysis in lipids dataset (LPOS)

The aim of this pilot study is to compare three microsampling devices profiles
with conventional liquid samples, plasma and whole blood, in an untargeted LCMS
analysis. The final objective is to determine which device perform a similar
profile to plasma, gold standard sample, generating less artefacts. For this,
there will be a qualitative comparison (number of features, metabolites
annotated) and a semi-quantitative comparison (intensity and statistical
differences).

*Samples of study*

The samples used in this study were venous blood samples collected from 10
healthy individuals ( 5 men and 5 women) in EDTA tubes. Part of this blood was
used as whole blood sample, part was used for plasma extraction and part was
spiked in devices to simulate sample collection through finger-prick.

Total number of samples: 66

Double blanks: 4

-   BLANK_001(beginning)

-   BLANK_002 (beginning)

-   BLANK_003 (beginning)

-   BLANK_END

Blanks from devices: 3

-   Blank_W

-   Blank_M

-   Blank_C

Randomized Samples: 60

-   10 x WHATMAN

-   10 x MITRA

-   10 x CAPITAINER

-   10 x PLASMA

-   10 x BLOOD

-   10 x QCs

*Sample preparation and acquisition*

Samples were treated with H2O and IPA for protein precipitation and lipids
extraction. Then extracts were analysed in a UPLC-ESI-QTOF system (Synapt-G2S)
(Waters Corporation) using TOFMS resolution mode for MS1 and FastDDA in
resolution moed for MS2 data.
